## What Ryo changes in the PRD

1. **Lead with team formation / internal mobility, not the overseas-exchange decision alone.** The exchange decision can stay as the demo case, but the problem line should be "choosing who goes where is done from memory and gut feel." That's the framing Ryo understood straight away. His example works on stage: HR needs one engineer who knows HR, not two more HR hires, and nobody can find that person today.
2. **The subject side is stronger than we thought.** Ryo already does by hand what the subject view does automatically: collecting his own receipts because his boss "didn't see" the work. Add a beat or a line: *the employee gets their own evidence deck for free*. That turns surveillance into a benefit for the employee, which answers the "creepy" objection.
3. **Demote the drift chain.** Ryo couldn't picture when chain-of-command drift happens ("I'm not clear on what is actually the scenarios"). The PRD already flags the VP's confirmation as led (§2, P2). Two of the three people we talked to didn't raise it unprompted, so it shouldn't open the demo.
4. **Peer perception is a gap we don't cover.** Ryo's real wish is showing his boss how his team's view of him changed over the half year, and the 360 free text is empty. Receipts reads what people wrote, not what they think. Say so if asked; don't claim it.
