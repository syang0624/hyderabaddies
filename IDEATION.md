## Slack (09/25 @ 7:30 PM) — mentor research signal

**Ryo Tomiki** (Product Owner, Indeed PLUS AI Assistant; part-time SMB recruiter in Japan) — DM thread with Carl, 09/25:

> Carl's pitch to Ryo: travel desk agent for small Japanese companies with no travel desk. Lives in Slack — type "大阪出張、10/8〜10/9、梅田で打ち合わせ", agent reads the company's travel policy (or, if none exists, asks the boss three questions once and writes the policy), compares shinkansen vs flight + hotel, books within policy, rebooks automatically if the meeting moves. Jalan as hotel supply.

Ryo's answer (paraphrased from his reply, caveated as "answering by imagination" — his own client base is service-industry SMBs, not ones with real travel volume):
- **Who books travel at a 20–50 person company?** Most SMBs don't even have a full-time admin person — even hiring is done by an employee moonlighting on it. If there *is* an admin doing it, it's manual/by-hand. This is the happy-path case, not the median.
- **Is there a written travel policy?** Almost never, unlike enterprise. It's "ask the boss." This makes SMBs *easier* to change than enterprise, since there's no policy to work around — just the boss's judgment call.

**Implication for the pitch:** the "no travel desk, ask the boss" framing is validated directly by someone who works SMB HR/ops in Japan. The "agent asks the boss 3 questions once, writes the policy" feature in the ideation doc lines up with this — worth leading with in the pitch as the actual insight, not just a nice-to-have feature.
