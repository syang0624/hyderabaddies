# Pik · One Screen — final buildable spec (Sat Sep 26 ~21:15 PDT; build target 05:00 Sun; deadline 11:00)

## 0. Thesis

The screen a team shares in Google Meet when someone asks "who fits this?" and the HR admin surface are one composed document: the Meet is that document being written live, the dashboard is it at rest. It replaces Hiro's calibration room (managers pitching from memory, "easily 4 or 5 days per quarter", INTERVIEW_NOTES 13-40 03:49–06:08) with receipts on the wall, and it is the one thing the VP endorsed (evidence made viewable so evaluators decide, 0:55:30–0:56:22) in the card shape he volunteered (a statement, then the exchanges that show it, 0:56:39). Nobody starts their day in it (PRD §2): every entry is a why (a Meet sentence, a Slack `?q=&focus=` link, an HR edit). Tone: Carl's dark monochrome, "very clear instead of very flashy" (INTERVIEW_NOTES:196). Grafts from Brief and Constellation are named where they land.

Definition, in engine.ask only (Slack and Notion inherit): `asked` = the ask's content terms after `_terms()`, with the stop list extended by `own instead hold just have they actually also good really anyone` and a phrase map (`push back`→pushback, `hold their own`→holdown), stems mapped back to surface words. `covered` = asked terms found in the person's own evidence: their will line (wcm-<id>, or will-<id> for generated people) or a receipt they authored (slack, docs, opted-in sessions). Manager paraphrases and skill tags do not count: a tag without a receipt is a bare tag; a paraphrase is not the person's words. `confidence = round(100 · covered / asked)`; ties are ties; 100 only when every word has a receipt. Never aggregated, never in the memo, never sorted by; `/api/ask` with `view=subject` returns 403. `?nonum=1` renders bar-only (D9 as written).
