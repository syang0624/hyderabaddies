# Interview Cheat Sheet — hyderabaddies

_Glance at this mid-conversation. Don't read it out loud._

**Theme reminder:** "future of work" = everything work-related (individuals, teams, orgs, labor, society), not just Recruit's own businesses. Judged 40% impact / 30% creativity / 30% technical — a real pain point for a clear person beats a cool idea for a fuzzy one.

**Golden rule: pain before pitch.** Don't mention the travel agent (or any idea) until step 5. If you lead with your idea, you get politeness, not truth.

---

### 1 — Who are you
Role, team size, who you work with day-to-day.

### 2 — Tell me the last time
"Walk me through the last time you [did the core thing their role does]."
→ Real memory, not hypothetical. Hypotheticals = what they *think* they do.

### 3 — Where'd it get annoying
"Where in that did it get slow / confusing?"
"What did you have to double-check, redo, or wait on someone for?"

### 4 — How do you cope today
"How do you deal with that right now?"
→ Elaborate hacky workaround = real pain. Shrug = not painful enough.

### 5 — Magic wand
"If you could skip one part of that, which part?"
→ Sharper now than as an opener, because it's anchored to a real story.

### 6 — Reveal (now, not before)
Frame as a question, not a pitch: "We're exploring an agent that [does X] — does that land in what you just described, or miss you entirely?"

### 7 — Close / next intro
"Who else hits this — or hits something totally different?"

---

**Watch for:** politeness answers ("yeah that'd be cool"), hypothetical-only answers, no real workaround mentioned = weak signal. Trust stories, not opinions.
