## 4. What this means for the 2:30 gate

Both documents point at the **same single fact**: what work is left after Claude produces the profile, how often, and with what consequence. Round 10's reversal condition is "observing a frequent, consequential, authorized checking step remaining after Shion's Claude workflow — one that an improved template does not remove — would move A ahead." Vouch's Module 1 is that bet made in advance. So the lunch observation in 01 is not a detour from Vouch; it is the only thing that can make Vouch's opening slide true.

If she shows that step: Module 1 plus the two-tier approval demo is path A with the strongest evidence in the room, and the deck says "one observed handoff", not "40 per cycle". If she says Claude already covers it: A1's kill criterion fires, Module 2 is what remains of Vouch, and its own kill criterion ("existing list handles nearly all cases") needs one more question before you build.

Either way the demo spine is the one both documents already agree on: real action, consequence, change the condition, different and explained consequence.
