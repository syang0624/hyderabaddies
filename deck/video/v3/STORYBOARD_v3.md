## What changed since V2 (Steven's feedback, relayed by Carl 23:35)

- Music under the whole cut, ducked by an envelope built from the narration offsets (150 ms down before a line, 500 ms up after), so it never competes with a word. Source: Carl's pick, not generated (Lyria was probed then dropped at the coordinator's instruction).
- "Two hundred people. Always current." → "Two hundred people, or twenty thousand. Always current." / caption "Any size. Always current."
- The English point is said plainly over the receipts (the line above), with the receipts on screen as it is said: own words at 6.6 s of the beat, Okada's note at 10.6 s, exactly when "Her manager wrote" is spoken.
- Narration re-rendered at 1.30x (Charon) and 1.20x (Puck), shortest valid take of two or three per line, and every beat starts on its first word. The Live model's pace varies per call (one 26-word line came back at 0.67 s, truncated; another at 8.3 s): `narrate_v3.py --takes N --keep-shorter` keeps the fastest complete take and rejects anything under 0.17 s/word.
- The Meet beat is the new Pik screen (Choreography A, UI-SPEC §8) and the new screen appears a second time as the admin page with the CRUD moment (Choreography B, §9), plus the mirror beat. The beat is **20.3 s, not the spec's 19.0**: the two manager lines (6.9 s + 4.3 s) and the new 26-word narration (8.3 s) do not fit 19 s without voices overlapping, so the spec's row times were re-derived from the measured wavs (`capture_ui.py:meet_rows`), the "Actually, just someone good" line was left out of the 90-second cut (nobody speaks it there; it stays in the stage video), and the manager's closing line is the shorter variant. The 8.5 s + 1.3 s came out of the animated beats (blur, asked, asks, receipts, verticals, end trimmed to their narration; mining at 1.3x, asks at 1.15x, verticals at 1.1x) and the Meet beat's holds; the two real takes are untouched.

## Rebuild

```bash
deck/video/pw/bin/python deck/video/v3/capture_ui.py            # starts (or reuses) this checkout's server on :8793; records meet, crud, stage, mirror -> captures/
GOOGLE_APPLICATION_CREDENTIALS=~/.config/carl-life-os/gcloud-tmuc/application_default_credentials.json \
  prototype/.venv/bin/python deck/video/v3/narrate_v3.py --takes 2 --keep-shorter [--only 07]   # narration -> vo/
python3 deck/video/v3/music_bed.py                                 # music/kosmose_vaikus.m4a -> music/bed.wav (calmest 96 s, -16 LUFS)
deck/video/pw/bin/python deck/video/v3/render.py 11               # the end card with the credit -> renders/11_end.mp4
python3 deck/video/v3/build_v3.py                                  # the stitch (needs PIL; numpy makes the envelope fast); writes TIMING.md
python3 deck/video/v3/build_stage_v3.py                            # meet_stage_demo.mp4 + STAGE_CUES.md from captures/stage_v3.webm
```

The captures re-time themselves from the wavs: after any narration change, re-run `capture_ui.py --beats meet` before `build_v3.py`. When the UI redesign lands (same event contract and URLs), the whole product-page side of the cut is `capture_ui.py` then the two builds; nothing is hand-animated.
