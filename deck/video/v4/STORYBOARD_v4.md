## The brand (one, the screen's)

Tokens copied from `prototype/ui/index.html` `:root` into `../v2/html/assets/shared.css`, used by every animated beat: page `#fafafa`, card `#ffffff` with the stacked hairline shadow `0 0 0 1px #00000014, 0 1px 1px #00000005, 0 2px 2px #0000000a`, soft `#f5f5f5`, ink `#171717`, body `#4d4d4d`, mute `#888888`, hairline `#ebebeb`, land `#e6e6e6` (dividers), ONE accent lime `#D6F25A` (mascot eyes and disc, the end card's highlight), lime-deep `#bfe030` (underlines, the receipt rings), lime-tint `#f4fbd6`, red `#ee0000` / red-soft `#f7d4d6` only for dropped or never-read. Geist, weight 600 max. The mascot is the screen's `#pikmark` (ink body, short antenna curling right, lime eyes), on a lime disc where it stands as an avatar. The old bone / sand / espresso / clay palette is gone from every beat; the two transparent overlays composited on the real Slack and Notion takes (`08_slack_real_pik`, `09_notion_real_pik`) keep their V2 mascot with its bone rim so it reads on the dark takes.

Music: Kosmose Vaikus by Kevin MacLeod, incompetech.com, CC BY 4.0, exactly as in V3 (the calmest 96 s, -26 dB under speech, -18 dB in gaps, 2 s in, 3 s out; the credit is on the end card).

Legend: **animated** = a V2 HTML beat (`../v2/html/`, rendered by `../v2/render.py` into `../v2/renders/`; all re-rendered for V4), plus `../v3/html/11_end.html` for the end card; **replayed** = the rebuilt Pik screen driven through the real compose/patch path (`prototype/live_script.py`), recorded headless at 1920x1080 by `../v3/capture_ui.py`; **driven** = the real page with the typing scripted; **real take** = Carl's recordings of the real Slack workspace and the real Notion board with the Pik mascot composited (V2, unchanged). Narration: `../v3/vo` (Gemini 3.8 Live on Vertex, Charon 1.30x, Puck 1.20x).
