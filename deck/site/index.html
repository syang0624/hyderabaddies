A2 Is that a score?

No. It is coverage of the ask by a person’s own receipts.

The engine computes

coverage = words of the ask with a receipt from this person ÷ words in the ask

Never

Sorted into a rank, or shown to the person as a number. Tiles are listed A to Z.

prototype/engine.py, coverage(): “2 of 7 words in the ask have a receipt from this person”, per ask only

Is that a score? No. “2 of 7 words have a receipt” is coverage of the ask by that person’s own receipts, never sorted by, never shown to the person as a rank. It changes with the ask, not with the person.

To Jim: coverage is per ask, so there is no standing profile to game. To Robert: the person is never shown a number about themselves. To Damien: the computation is deterministic code over the model’s cited claims. To Ho Joon: this is the design decision that removes the ranking incentive from the model.
